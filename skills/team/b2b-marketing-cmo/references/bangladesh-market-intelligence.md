## Business Culture

### Decision-Making Characteristics

| Factor | Reality | Implication |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **Relationship-driven** | Trust before transaction | Long relationship-building phase |
| **Hierarchy matters** | Senior approval required | Must reach C-suite |
| **Personal networks** | Referrals highly valued | Leverage CEO connections |
| **Face-to-face preferred** | Digital + physical | Don't over-rely on email |
| **Negotiation expected** | Price discussions normal | Build in negotiation room |

### Sales Cycle Realities

| Segment | Typical Cycle | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| SME (50-200 emp) | 2-3 months | Faster decisions, fewer stakeholders |
| Mid-market (200-500 emp) | 3-5 months | Multiple approvals |
| Enterprise (500+ emp) | 6-12 months | Procurement, committee decisions |
| MNCs | 4-8 months | Regional approval often needed |

### Budget Cycles

| Sector | Fiscal Year | Budget Planning |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Government | July-June | Jan-Mar planning |
| Most corporates | Jan-Dec | Oct-Nov planning |
| MNCs | Varies by parent | Often Oct-Dec |
| NGOs/Development | Varies | Project-based |

## Competitive Advantage

### "Can approach almost everyone in the country"

**What this means:**
- CEO network provides warm introductions
- Relationship access to major corporates
- Bypass cold outreach challenges
- Trust established before pitch

**How to leverage:**
1. Map CEO network to target accounts
2. Request specific introductions
3. Position as peer-to-peer conversation
4. Follow up relationship with marketing support

### Local vs. Global Positioning

| Factor | | Global Competitors |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| Local support | ✓ Dhaka-based | Remote/regional |
| Bangladesh understanding | ✓ 13 years | Generic |
| Pricing | BDT, flexible | USD, rigid |
| Implementation | Weeks | Months |
| Relationship | Direct to CEO | Account managers |
