## CAC & LTV Calculations

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

```
CAC = (Marketing Spend + Sales Spend) / New Customers Acquired

Monthly CAC = (Monthly Marketing + Allocated Sales Cost) / New Customers
```

**Target CAC by Product:**

| Product | Target CAC | Max CAC | Rationale |
|---------|------------|---------|-----------|
| Proggya | BDT 50,000 | BDT 80,000 | Monthly subscription model |
| ICE | BDT 100,000 | BDT 150,000 | Campaign-based, higher ticket |
| Forge | BDT 75,000 | BDT 120,000 | Mid-size deals |
| TradeEye | BDT 500,000 | BDT 1,000,000 | Enterprise, high LTV |

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

```
LTV = Average Monthly Revenue × Gross Margin × Average Customer Lifespan (months)
```

**Estimated LTV by Product:**

| Product | Avg Monthly Rev | Margin | Lifespan | LTV |
|---------|-----------------|--------|----------|-----|
| Proggya | BDT 75,000 | 70% | 24 mo | BDT 1,260,000 |
| ICE | BDT 200,000 | 60% | 12 mo | BDT 1,440,000 |
| Forge | BDT 100,000 | 65% | 18 mo | BDT 1,170,000 |
| TradeEye | BDT 2,000,000 | 55% | 36 mo | BDT 39,600,000 |

### LTV:CAC Ratio

**Target: >3:1**

| Product | Target LTV:CAC | Minimum |
