## 2. Channel Strategy Matrix

### Channel Effectiveness for Bangladesh B2B

| Channel | Reach | Cost | Effort | Best For | Priority |
|---------|-------|------|--------|----------|---------------------|
| **LinkedIn (Organic)** | Medium | Low | Medium | Thought leadership, credibility | HIGH |
| **LinkedIn (Paid)** | High | Medium | Medium | Targeted awareness, lead gen | MEDIUM |
| **WhatsApp Direct** | Low-Medium | Low | High | Relationship nurture, quick updates | HIGH |
| **Industry Events** | Medium | High | High | Relationship building, demos | HIGH |
| **Email Marketing** | Medium | Low | Medium | Nurture, announcements | MEDIUM |
| **Cold Calling** | Low | Medium | High | Enterprise outreach (TradeEye) | LOW-MEDIUM |
| **Website/SEO** | Low (BD) | Medium | High | Credibility, inbound | LOW |
| **Industry Associations** | Low-Medium | Low-Medium | Medium | Network, credibility | MEDIUM |
| **Partner Referrals** | Low-Medium | Low | Medium | Warm introductions | HIGH |
| **PR/Media** | Medium | Medium | Medium | Major announcements | LOW |

### Channel Selection by Campaign Type

| Campaign Type | Primary Channels | Secondary Channels |
|---------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Product Launch** | Events, LinkedIn, Email, PR | Partner marketing |
| **Lead Generation** | LinkedIn Paid, Direct outreach, Events | Email, Content |
| **Thought Leadership** | LinkedIn Organic, Events (speaking), Email | PR |
| **ABM (Enterprise)** | Direct outreach, Events, Custom content | LinkedIn |
| **Brand Awareness** | LinkedIn, Events, PR, Industry associations | Content |
| **Customer Expansion** | Email, Direct outreach, WhatsApp | Events |

### Channel Mix by Product

| Product | Primary | Secondary | Avoid |
|---------|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Proggya + Data Dialogue** | LinkedIn, Events, Email | Content, Partner | Cold calling |
| **ICE** | Events, LinkedIn, Case studies | Partner, Direct | SEO |
| **Forge** | LinkedIn, Email, Training events | HR associations | Mass marketing |
| **TradeEye** | Direct outreach, Executive events | LinkedIn (targeted) | Broad campaigns |

## 4. Campaign Type Playbooks

### Product Launch Campaign

```
PRODUCT LAUNCH PLAYBOOK
═══════════════════════

Timeline: 12 weeks (4 weeks pre-launch, launch week, 7 weeks post-launch)

PRE-LAUNCH (Weeks -4 to -1)
───────────────────────────

Week -4: Foundation
□ Finalize product positioning and messaging
□ Create buyer personas and journey maps
□ Develop pricing and packaging
□ Begin sales enablement materials

Week -3: Content Creation
□ Product overview video/demo
□ Launch blog post
□ FAQ document
□ Sales deck and battle cards
□ Email templates for sales

Week -2: Collateral & Setup
□ Landing page live
□ Press release drafted
□ Social media content scheduled
□ Email sequences loaded
□ Event registrations open (if applicable)

Week -1: Final Prep
□ Internal team briefing
□ Partner/stakeholder notification
□ Final content review
□ Tracking and analytics setup
□ Launch day checklist confirmed


LAUNCH WEEK (Week 0)
────────────────────

Launch Day:
□ Press release distributed
□ Email to customer base
□ LinkedIn announcement (company page)
□ Executive posts on LinkedIn
□ Website updated
□ Sales team actively outreaching

Launch Week Activities:
□ Daily social media posts
□ Sales team blitz
□ Follow-up on initial inquiries
□ Media/influencer outreach
□ Monitor and respond to feedback


POST-LAUNCH (Weeks 1-7)
───────────────────────

Weeks 1-2: Momentum
□ Second wave of content (case study, deeper dive)
□ Webinar or demo event
□ Targeted follow-up with warm leads
□ Collect and share early customer feedback

Weeks 3-4: Sustained Push
□ Customer testimonials/quotes
□ Industry-specific use cases
□ Partnership announcements (if any)
□ Performance review and optimization

Weeks 5-7: Long-tail
□ Ongoing content drumbeat
□ Lead nurturing sequences active
□ Sales follow-through
□ Post-mortem and lessons learned


SUCCESS METRICS

| Launch day website traffic | | |
| Email open rate | | |
| Demo requests week 1 | | |
| Leads generated month 1 | | |
| Pipeline created | | |
| Deals closed month 1 | | |
```

### Account-Based Marketing Campaign (for TradeEye)

```
ABM CAMPAIGN PLAYBOOK
═════════════════════

Target Accounts: [#] named accounts
Timeline: 16 weeks

PHASE 1: ACCOUNT SELECTION & RESEARCH (Weeks 1-2)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────

□ Define Ideal Customer Profile
□ Build target account list (Tier 1, Tier 2)
□ Research each account:
  - Company overview and news
  - Key stakeholders and org chart
  - Current technology stack
  - Business challenges and priorities

─────────────────────────────────────────────

□ Proposal development for engaged accounts
```

## Output Checklist

Before launching any campaign:

- [ ] Campaign brief complete and approved
- [ ] Target audience clearly defined
- [ ] Key messages documented
- [ ] Channel mix selected with budget allocation
- [ ] Content calendar created
- [ ] All creative assets developed
- [ ] Landing pages/forms live and tested
- [ ] Tracking and analytics configured
- [ ] Team briefed on roles and responsibilities
- [ ] Success metrics defined with targets
- [ ] Post-campaign review scheduled
