## 3. Pitch Scripts

### 60-Second Elevator Pitch Template

```
ELEVATOR PITCH: [Product Name]
══════════════════════════════

HOOK (10 seconds)
─────────────────
"Did you know that [surprising statistic or question related to their pain point]?"

PROBLEM (10 seconds)
────────────────────
"Most [target audience] struggle with [core problem], leading to 
[negative consequence]."

SOLUTION (15 seconds)
─────────────────────
"[Product name] is a [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [alternative], 
we [key differentiator]."

PROOF (10 seconds)
──────────────────
"We're already helping [reference customer] achieve [specific result]."

CTA (10 seconds)
────────────────
"I'd love to show you how this could work for [their company]. Can we 
schedule a 30-minute demo?"
```

### Discovery Call Script

```
DISCOVERY CALL FRAMEWORK
════════════════════════

Duration: 30 minutes
Goal: Qualify opportunity, understand needs, set next steps

OPENING (2 minutes)
───────────────────
"Thank you for making time today. Before we dive in, I'd love to 
understand your world a bit better. My goal is to see if there's 
a fit between what we do and what you need—and if there isn't, 
I'll tell you honestly. Does that work for you?"


CURRENT STATE QUESTIONS (10 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────

Business Context:
• "Tell me about your role and what success looks like for you this year."
• "What are the top 2-3 priorities for your team right now?"

Problem Exploration:
• "When it comes to [relevant area], what's working well?"
• "What's frustrating about the current approach?"
• "If you could wave a magic wand, what would be different?"

Current Solutions:
• "How are you handling [problem] today?"
• "What have you tried that didn't work?"
• "What tools or systems are you using?"


IMPACT QUESTIONS (5 minutes)
────────────────────────────

• "What happens when [problem] doesn't get solved?"
• "Can you quantify the impact? Time, money, opportunities missed?"
• "Who else is affected by this?"


SOLUTION OVERVIEW (8 minutes)
─────────────────────────────

"Based on what you've shared, let me show you how we've helped 
similar companies..."

[Tailored pitch based on their answers]
[Show relevant case study or demo]


NEXT STEPS (5 minutes)
──────────────────────

Buying Process:
• "Besides yourself, who else would need to be involved in evaluating this?"
• "What does your typical timeline look like for decisions like this?"
• "Is there budget allocated for this type of solution?"

Close:
• "What would be the most valuable next step for you?"
• "Shall we schedule a deeper technical demo with your team?"
```

## 6. Communication Guidelines

### Tone of Voice

| Attribute | Do | Don't |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| **Confident** | State expertise clearly | Oversell or exaggerate |
| **Approachable** | Use conversational language | Use jargon unnecessarily |
| **Helpful** | Focus on customer value | Focus on features |
| **Professional** | Maintain business appropriateness | Be stiff or formal |
| **Local** | Reference Bangladesh context | Ignore local nuances |

### Language Guidelines

**Power Words for Team:**
- Transform, Enable, Accelerate
- Insight, Intelligence, Visibility
- Partner, Collaborate, Support
- Proven, Trusted, Reliable
- Results, Impact, Outcome

**Words to Avoid:**
- Revolutionary, Disruptive (overused)
- Cutting-edge, World-class (cliché)
- Synergy, Leverage (corporate jargon)
- Cheap, Discount (value, not cheap)
- Simple, Easy (implies customer problem is trivial)

## Quality Checklist

Before sending any communication:

- [ ] Clear, benefit-focused subject line
- [ ] Personalized opening (not generic)
- [ ] Value proposition relevant to recipient
- [ ] Proof points included
- [ ] Clear call to action
- [ ] Professional tone, no typos
- [ ] Mobile-friendly formatting
- [ ] Appropriate length for channel
- [ ] Contact information included
- [ ] Compliance with any regulations
