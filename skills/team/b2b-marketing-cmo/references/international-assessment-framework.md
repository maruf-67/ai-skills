## Entry Mode Options

| Mode | Investment | Time | Control | Risk | Best For |
|------|------------|------|---------|------|----------|
| Remote Delivery | Low | Fast | High | Low | Testing market, services |
| Local Partner/Reseller | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium | Quick scale, local presence |
| Sales Rep | Medium | Medium | High | Medium | Direct control, learning |
| Local Office | High | Slow | High | High | Commitment, scale |

### Recommended Sequence

```
Year 1: Remote + Partner
   • Deliver from Bangladesh
   • Partner for sales/marketing
   • Test product-market fit

Year 2: Expand Presence
   • Add sales rep if traction
   • Increase partner investment
   • Develop local case studies

Year 3+: Office Decision
   • If revenue justifies
   • For client proximity
   • As regional hub
```

## Go/No-Go Checklist

Before entering any market:

### Minimum Requirements
- [ ] At least one referenceable client or pilot
- [ ] Product localization (language, currency, compliance)
- [ ] Local partner or sales channel identified
- [ ] 12-month investment budget allocated
- [ ] Pricing validated for market
- [ ] CEO time commitment available

### Ideal Conditions
- [ ] 2+ clients or strong pipeline
- [ ] Local presence (office or dedicated partner)
- [ ] 3+ reference customers in market
- [ ] Break-even path within 18 months
- [ ] Strategic alignment with 5-year plan

## International Marketing Adjustments

| Element | Bangladesh | International |
|---------|------------|---------------|
| Pricing | BDT, flexible | USD/EUR/AED, more structure |
| Sales cycle | Relationship-first | May be more transactional |
| Channels | Events, WhatsApp | LinkedIn, digital, events |
| Messaging | Local context | Industry universal + local |
| Case studies | BATB, local brands | Need international references |
| Support | Same timezone | May need extended hours |
