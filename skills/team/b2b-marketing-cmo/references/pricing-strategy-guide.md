# Pricing Strategy Guide

Value-based pricing methodology, competitive benchmarking, and model selection for products.

---

## 1. Pricing Strategy Framework

### Strategic Pricing Principles

1. **Price to Value, Not to Cost:** Base pricing on customer value created, not delivery costs
2. **Segment-Based Pricing:** Different customer segments may warrant different pricing
3. **Model Alignment:** Pricing model should align with how customers receive value
4. **Competitive Context:** Understand but don't blindly follow competitor pricing
5. **Flexibility for Bangladesh:** Build in negotiation room for relationship-driven market

### Pricing Model Selection Matrix

| Model | Best For | Products | Pros | Cons |
|-------|----------|---------------------|------|------|
| **Monthly Subscription** | Continuous value, predictable usage | Proggya | Predictable revenue, customer loyalty | Churn risk, lower upfront |
| **Annual Subscription** | Committed relationships | Proggya, Data Dialogue | Higher commitment, cash flow | Larger upfront ask |
| **Usage-Based** | Variable consumption | Data Dialogue (queries) | Fair for light users, scales with value | Revenue unpredictability |
| **Campaign/Project** | Discrete deliverables | ICE | Clear scope, high ticket | Revenue lumpy, reselling effort |
| **Per-Seat** | User-specific tools | Forge (per trainee) | Scales with adoption | May limit expansion |
| **Enterprise License** | Large deployments | TradeEye | High deal value | Long sales cycles |
| **Hybrid** | Complex solutions | TradeEye (license + support) | Captures multiple value streams | Complexity |

## 2. Value-Based Pricing Methodology

### Step 1: Identify Customer Value

**Value Categories:**

| Category | Description | Measurement |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Revenue Increase** | Helps customer make more money | $ additional revenue |
| **Cost Reduction** | Helps customer spend less | $ savings |
| **Risk Mitigation** | Protects customer from losses | $ avoided loss × probability |
| **Time Savings** | Frees up customer time | Hours saved × hourly rate |
| **Strategic Value** | Enables new capabilities | Harder to quantify |

### Step 3: Validate Against Willingness to Pay

**Validation Sources:**
- RFP budgets from pipeline
- Past deal data
- Competitive pricing
- Customer conversations
- Industry benchmarks


### Research Protocol

**Step 1: Identify Comparables**
```

════════════════════════════

Category: [Product category]

| Competitor | Product | Pricing Model | Price Range |
|------------|---------|---------------|-------------|
| [Comp 1] | [Product] | [Model] | [Range] |
| [Comp 2] | [Product] | [Model] | [Range] |
| [Comp 3] | [Product] | [Model] | [Range] |

Indirect Competitors / Alternatives:
| Alternative | Description | Cost to Customer |
|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| [Alt 1] | [Description] | [Cost] |
| [Alt 2] | [Description] | [Cost] |
```

**Step 2: Normalize Pricing**

Since competitors may use different models, normalize to comparable units:
- Convert to monthly equivalent
- Calculate per-user or per-unit cost
- Factor in implementation/setup costs

**Step 3: Position Decision**

| Position | Strategy | When to Use |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Premium** | 20-50% above market | Strong differentiation, brand strength |
| **Competitive** | Within 10% of market | Undifferentiated, price-sensitive segment |
| **Value** | 20-30% below market | Market entry, share capture |
| **Penetration** | 50%+ below market | Disruptive entry, lock-in strategy |

## 4. Pricing Tiers (Good-Better-Best)

### Tier Design Principles

1. **Anchor with Premium:** Lead with highest tier to anchor expectations
2. **Target the Middle:** Design "Better" tier as the intended purchase
3. **Clear Upgrade Path:** Make it obvious what you get by moving up
4. **Remove Friction:** Basic tier should satisfy core need without frustration

   - Search: "corporate training software pricing"

   - Extract budget signals from 3 current RFPs
   - Understand scope and scale of each

**Pricing Model Recommendation Framework:**

| Model Option | When to Use | Pricing Structure |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------|

| **Hybrid** | Platform + content development | License + project fees |

### TradeEye Pricing Research Protocol

**Current Status:** Highly variable deal sizes (BDT 20M-160M), needs pricing framework

**Research Required:**

1. **Field Force Management Software Pricing**
   - Search: "field force automation software pricing"
   - Search: "merchandising software licensing"
   - Search: "retail execution platform cost"

2. **Calculate Value-Based Ceiling**
   - Quantify: Sales increase from better merchandising
   - Quantify: Cost savings from automated verification

| **Large** (500+ field staff) | Enterprise + Success | License + managed services |
| **Strategic** (National rollout) | Partnership | Revenue share or outcome-based |

## 6. Discounting Policy

### Discount Authority Matrix

| Discount Level | Approval Required | When Appropriate |
|----------------|-------------------|------------------|
| 0-5% | Sales Representative | Early payment, minor negotiation |
| 5-10% | Sales Manager | Competitive pressure, volume |
| 10-15% | Director/VP | Strategic account, multi-year |
| 15-20% | CEO | Exceptional circumstances, lighthouse |
| 20%+ | Not recommended | Document strong justification |

### Value-Add Alternatives to Discounting

Instead of price reduction, consider:
- Extended payment terms
- Additional training/onboarding
- Extended trial period
- Feature upgrades at same price
- Multi-year lock at current price
- Implementation support included
- Premium support at standard price

## 7. Bangladesh Market Considerations

### Pricing Realities

| Factor | Implication | Strategy |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Relationship-driven** | Price is negotiable | Build in negotiation room (10-15%) |
| **Budget cycles** | Fiscal year constraints | Flexible timing, annual options |
| **Cash flow concerns** | Monthly may be preferred | Offer monthly/quarterly options |
| **USD volatility** | Currency risk | BDT pricing, periodic review |
| **Tax implications** | VAT, withholding | Quote inclusive/exclusive clearly |

### Payment Term Templates

**Standard Terms:**
- Net 30 days from invoice
- Quarterly advance for subscriptions
- Annual prepayment with 10% discount

**Flexible Terms (for large deals):**
- Monthly billing available
- Milestone-based for projects
- Deferred start for budget timing

### Price Communication

**Do:**
- Lead with value, then price
- Present as investment with ROI
- Offer comparison to alternatives
- Include payment flexibility

**Don't:**
- Lead with price
- Discount too quickly
- Anchor too low initially
- Ignore competitive context
