## Proggya + Data Dialogue

### Product Summary
AI-powered BI platform with natural language interface. Users ask business questions in plain English, get instant visualizations.

### Ideal Customer Profile
- **Company**: MNCs, large local corporates, research institutions
- **Size**: 200+ employees
- **Industries**: Any with significant data (FMCG, pharma, research, banking)
- **Pain**: Manual reporting, scattered data, IT bottlenecks for insights

### Primary Buyer Personas

| Persona | Title | Goals | Pain Points |
|---------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Data-Driven Director | COO, CFO, Director | Better decisions faster | Can't trust numbers, slow reports |
| Overwhelmed Analyst | Business Analyst | Focus on insights not wrangling | Stuck doing manual work |
| Strategic IT Lead | IT Director, CIO | Reduce shadow IT, enable users | BI tool unused, support burden |

### Key Differentiators
1. Natural language queries (no SQL)
2. 4-week deployment
3. Local support in Bangladesh
4. Proven with BATB, Oxford

### Q1 Marketing Focus
- Thought leadership: "Data-driven decision making in Bangladesh"
- Case study development
- LinkedIn content targeting C-suite

## ICE (Gamified Consumer Engagement)

### Product Summary
Platform for creating gamified consumer engagement campaigns. Instant-win mechanics, first-party data capture, multi-channel deployment.

### Ideal Customer Profile
- **Company**: Consumer brands, retailers
- **Size**: 100+ employees, marketing budget
- **Industries**: FMCG, pharma (OTC), beverages, real estate
- **Pain**: Low engagement rates, rising media costs, weak first-party data

### Primary Buyer Personas

| Persona | Title | Goals | Pain Points |
|---------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Brand Builder | Brand Manager | Cut through noise, build loyalty | Traditional campaigns flat |
| Data Hunter | Digital Marketing Lead | Build first-party data asset | Cookie deprecation, no owned data |
| Event Activator | Activation Manager | Make events memorable | Low event ROI, weak follow-up |

### Key Differentiators
1. 40%+ participation rates
2. First-party data capture built-in
3. Multi-platform (mobile, in-store, events)
4. 2M+ consumers already engaged

### Q1 Marketing Focus
- Event demonstrations
- Campaign case studies with ROI
- LinkedIn targeting brand managers

## Forge (Corporate Training Gamification)

### Product Summary
Gamified corporate training platform. Transforms compliance/skill training into engaging experiences with points, leaderboards, challenges.

### Ideal Customer Profile
- **Company**: Medium-large enterprises
- **Size**: 200+ employees, formal training function
- **Industries**: Any with significant training needs (manufacturing, pharma, banking, retail)
- **Pain**: Low training completion, poor retention, compliance risk

### Primary Buyer Personas

| Persona | Title | Goals | Pain Points |
|---------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Learning Leader | L&D Head, Training Manager | Improve training outcomes | Employees don't complete training |
| HR Strategist | CHRO, HR Director | Build capable workforce | Training seen as checkbox |
| Operations Chief | COO, Plant Manager | Reduce errors from poor training | Knowledge doesn't stick |

### Key Differentiators
1. Gamification proven to 3x completion rates
2. Custom content development available
3. Progress tracking and analytics
4. Mobile-friendly for field teams

### Q1 Marketing Focus
- HR event presence
- Training ROI content
- **Priority: Develop pricing model** (has 3 RFPs waiting)

### Pricing Research Needed
- Benchmark training software pricing
- Analyze RFP budgets
- Consider per-trainee vs. enterprise licensing

## TradeEye (AI Merchandising Verification)

### Product Summary
AI-powered field force and merchandising verification. Computer vision analyzes retail photos to verify compliance, provides real-time dashboards.

### Ideal Customer Profile
- **Company**: FMCG manufacturers with field teams
- **Size**: 50+ field reps, 5000+ retail touchpoints
- **Industries**: FMCG, beverages, tobacco, consumer electronics
- **Pain**: Blind at retail, manual audits limited, Perfect Store gaps

### Primary Buyer Personas

| Persona | Title | Goals | Pain Points |
|---------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Trade Champion | Trade Marketing Director | Perfect Store execution | Don't know what's on shelf |
| Field Commander | National Sales Manager | Team productivity, coverage | Manual reporting unreliable |
| Brand Protector | Marketing Director | Shelf visibility, brand blocking | Execution kills brand investment |

### Key Differentiators
1. 97% AI accuracy on SKU recognition
2. Bangladesh-optimized (trained on local retail)
3. Real-time dashboards
4. Live with BATB at national scale

### Q1 Marketing Focus
- ABM for top 10 FMCG targets
- Executive engagement (CEO-to-CEO)
- **Lowest marketing spend** (enterprise direct sales model)

### Pricing Research Needed
- Value-based pricing framework
- Deal sizing model (based on field reps, outlets, scope)
- Enterprise licensing vs. managed service options
