```

   □ Real Estate
   □ Research/Academia
   □ Defense/Government
   □ Banking/Financial Services
   □ Manufacturing
   □ Retail

   Company Size (by employees):
   □ Small: 10-50
   □ Medium: 51-200
   □ Large: 201-1000
   □ Enterprise: 1000+

   □ <BDT 10 crore

   □ Dhaka (Corporate HQs)

   □ Competitive pressure
```

### Positioning Template

```
POSITIONING STATEMENT
═════════════════════

For [target segment]
who [key pain point/need]
[Product name] is a [category]
that [key benefit/differentiator]
unlike [competitor/alternative]
our product [unique capability/proof point]

EXAMPLE:
For mid-sized FMCG companies in Bangladesh
who struggle with inconsistent merchandising execution,
TradeEye is an AI-powered field verification platform
that provides real-time compliance monitoring and analytics,
unlike manual auditing or basic photo-capture apps,
our product uses computer vision to automatically verify
90+ SKU placements with 97% accuracy.

POSITIONING ELEMENTS BREAKDOWN:

Target Segment: [who we serve]
Frame of Reference: [category we compete in]
Point of Difference: [unique value we provide]
Reason to Believe: [proof that we deliver]
Emotional Benefit: [how customers feel]
```

## Framework Selection Guide

| Situation | Primary Framework | Supporting |
|-----------|-------------------|------------|
| New product launch | 4Ps → STP | AIDA, Blue Ocean |
| Entering new market | PESTLE → Ansoff | STP, Porter's |
| Competitive response | Porter's 5 Forces | Positioning |
| Pricing decision | Value-based → Competitive | 4Ps (Price) |
| Campaign design | AIDA → Content map | STP |
| Growth strategy | Ansoff Matrix | Porter's, SWOT |
| Differentiation need | Blue Ocean | Positioning, STP |
