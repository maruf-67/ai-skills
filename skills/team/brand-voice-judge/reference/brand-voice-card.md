# Brand Voice Quick Reference Card

**Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** May 2026 | **Source:** Corporate Marketing Plan FY 2026–27, Section 1.3

Distribute to: all content creators, freelancers, and agency partners.

---

## Who We Are

**Softograph** is a Distribution Excellence Consultancy. We deploy AI as an instrument of client transformation. We are NOT a software vendor selling licenses.

**Brand posture:** The "wise guide." We attract senior decision-makers through demonstrated expertise, evidence of impact, and a clearly articulated worldview.

---

## Do / Avoid Matrix

| Dimension | ✅ Do | ❌ Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice** | Authoritative, measured, evidence-led | Eager, salesy, self-congratulatory |
| **Language** | Specific outcomes, named methodologies, client-impact framing | Buzzwords: disruptive, revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge, next-gen |
| **Proof** | Data points, named clients (with permission), evidence chains | Superlatives without evidence, feature lists, vanity metrics |
| **Posture** | "Through our work in emerging markets, we have observed..." | "We are the best / leading / only provider of..." |
| **CTA Style** | Invitation: "If this resonates, let's explore together." | Pressure: "Don't miss out / Limited time / Act now" |

---

## Banned Words

### Hard Ban — never use in any content
disruptive · revolutionary · seamless · cutting-edge · next-gen · next-generation · game-changing · world-class · best-in-class · "the best" · "the leading" · "the only" · "number one" · "#1" · "don't miss out" · "limited time" · "act now" · "hurry"

### Soft Ban — avoid unless context genuinely requires it
innovative · leverage · synergy · ecosystem · scalable · robust · state-of-the-art · end-to-end · one-stop · "excited to announce" · "thrilled to share" · "proud to"

## Product Framing Rule

Always describe products by the **business outcome**, never by technical features.

| Product | ✅ Say This | ❌ Not This |
|---|---|---|
| **Proggya** | Revenue intelligence that shows where money leaks before the quarter closes | A BI/analytics dashboard tool |
| **Data Dialogue** | Ask your data a question in plain language and get a boardroom-ready answer | A RAG-based AI interface |
| **TruMark** | Counterfeit detection that protects brand trust at the point of sale | An AI-powered image recognition system |
| **Inspecto** | Field verification that proves work happened — with evidence, not promises | A task verification app with image metadata |
| **Callibrio** | Grant management that auditors trust and researchers actually use | Research grant tracking software |
| **TradeEye** | Distribution visibility from factory to shelf — every route, every stop | A supply chain monitoring platform |

## Quick Self-Check

Before publishing, ask:

1. Would a thoughtful industry peer respect this tone? → If not, rewrite.
2. Does every claim have evidence or at least a specific reference? → If not, add or remove.
3. Would this embarrass us if a prospective client's CEO read it? → If yes, rewrite.
4. Are we selling or teaching? → We should be teaching. Always.
