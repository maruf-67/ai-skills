# Events & Networking

Covers Team's offline brand presence — events strategy, speaking opportunities, chamber engagement, networking outreach, and internal achievements documentation. The Offline Campaign pillar is often the most neglected; this skill ensures it stays active.

---

## Why Offline Matters for Team

Team's best clients often come through relationships, not inbound. A consistent offline presence — at the right events, with the right people — compounds over years. The goal is not to attend everything; it is to be visibly present at the events that matter, and to be known by the people who matter.

**Primary offline objectives:**
1. Be recognised as a credible AI/software company by industry bodies (BASIS, ICT Division)
2. Build relationships in business chambers (DCCI, MCCI, FBCCI, American Chamber)
3. Create speaking opportunities for Tawhid in technology and business forums
4. Enter and win relevant awards for credibility with enterprise clients
5. Document achievements that reinforce Team's brand story

---

## Priority Venues and Organisations

### Business Chambers (Highest Priority)
- **DCCI** (Dhaka Chamber of Commerce and Industry) — core local network
- **MCCI** (Metropolitan Chamber) — larger enterprise network
- **FBCCI** (Federation of Bangladesh Chambers) — national-level presence
- **American Chamber of Commerce in Bangladesh** — international business network
- **British High Commission business events** — connects to UK market (Callibrio fit)
- **EU Chamber** — connects to Spain/Portugal expansion plans

### Industry Bodies
- **BASIS** (Bangladesh Association of Software and Information Services) — most relevant tech body
- **ICT Division Bangladesh** — government-aligned, policy and recognition events
- **ISPAB** — IT sector association

### International (for expansion)
- **South Asian Association** events — regional visibility
- UAE-based tech/FMCG events (for Dubai network building)
- Nigeria-based tech events — TechCabal community, Lagos startup ecosystem

## Phase 1: Event Assessment

For any event under consideration, evaluate:

**Audience fit score (1-5):**
- 5: Decision-makers from Team's target sectors (FMCG, pharma, banking, research)
- 4: Technology industry peers and potential partners
- 3: Mixed business audience including potential clients
- 2: General business audience, minimal sector overlap
- 1: Unlikely to produce useful connections

**Participation options:**
| Option | Best when | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Attend | Building awareness, meeting targets | Low |
| Speak | Thought leadership, CEO visibility | Medium-High |
| Sponsor | Brand visibility at large events | High (cost) |
| Exhibit | Product demonstrations at tech events | High (cost+effort) |
| Submit award | Applicable category, strong evidence | Medium |

**Recommendation format:**

```
EVENT ASSESSMENT: [Event name]
──────────────────────────────
Date: [Date] | Location: [City/Online]
Organiser: [Who runs it]
Expected attendance: [If known]

Audience fit: [Score/5] — [Who attends]
Participation recommendation: [Attend / Speak / Sponsor / Skip]
Why: [Rationale]
Priority: [High / Normal / Low]
Registration deadline: [Date if known]
Estimated cost: [Attendance fee if known]
Cross-pillar benefit: [What content or PR comes from attending]
```

## Phase 2: Participation Plan

For approved events, define the objective and approach:

**Objective types:**
- **Relationship building** — attending to meet specific people or organisations
- **Thought leadership** — speaking to position Tawhid or as an expert
- **Business development** — meeting potential clients or partners
- **Award/recognition** — entering an award or attending a ceremony
- **Presence** — maintaining visibility in the industry community

**Approach per objective:**

*Relationship building:*
- Identify 5-10 target contacts to meet at the event
- Prepare a talking point for each (know what they care about)
- Plan a follow-up mechanism (LinkedIn connection, email, business card)

*Thought leadership:*
- Identify the speaking slot or panel most aligned to Tawhid's expertise
- Prepare the speaker proposal (see Phase 4)
- Plan how the talk will be repurposed (LinkedIn, blog, PR)

For any event Tawhid or the team is attending, prepare a brief:

- [ ] Business cards

## Phase 4: Speaker Proposal

When submitting a speaking proposal:

```
SPEAKING PROPOSAL: [Event name]

PROPOSED TOPIC: [Title — specific and outcome-oriented]

ABSTRACT (150-200 words):
[Opening with the problem or question the talk addresses]
[What the audience will learn]
[Tawhid's unique angle — specific to his experience building AI systems]
[Takeaway the audience will leave with]

SPEAKER BIO (80-100 words):
Tawhid Rahman is the CEO of Limited, a Dhaka-based AI and enterprise software company he founded in 2012. is ISO certified and serves enterprise clients including British American Tobacco Bangladesh. Tawhid has built AI-powered systems for FMCG, healthcare, banking, and research clients, and writes about practical AI development on Substack. He has [X years] of experience [specific relevant claim].

TOPIC AREAS (Tawhid's strongest speaking angles):
- Practical AI deployment in enterprise environments (not theory)
- Building AI systems that actually work in production
- The Bangladesh tech industry and what's coming next
- Corporate AI adoption: what companies get wrong
- From founder to AI company: building over 12 years
```

---

## Phase 5: Post-Event Plan

After any event, always produce:

1. **LinkedIn post** — key insight or observation from the event (Channel)
2. **Follow-up sequence** — personalised messages to contacts made (Networking)
3. **Internal debrief note** — who was met, what was discussed, next steps (Internal)
4. **Cross-pillar check** — does anything from the event feed PR, an award entry, or a blog post?

---

## Phase 6: Internal Achievements → External Brand Assets

Internal achievements are often missed as PR and brand opportunities. When something significant happens internally, it should be documented for external use.

**Achievement types to capture:**
- Team growth milestones (X employees, new senior hire)
- Certification achievements (ISO, partner certifications)
- Client portfolio milestones (X clients, Y years with a major client)
- Product milestones (X users, first deployment in a new sector)
- Award wins and nominations (even being shortlisted is worth communicating)

**Documentation format:**

```
ACHIEVEMENT: [What happened]
Date: [When]
Significance: [Why this matters — for sales, brand, or credibility]
Evidence: [Numbers, names, or certificates available]
External use: [LinkedIn post / Press release / Award entry / Sales material]
CEO angle: [Is there a personal reflection Tawhid can share?]
```

Once documented, pass to `cross-pillar-ideation` to map the full downstream opportunity.
